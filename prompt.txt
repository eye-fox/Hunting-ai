VALIDATION TARGET:
Findings: 2 vulnerabilities (VULN-001 Medium, VULN-002 Low)
notes: Two vulnerabilities identified in java-tron (tronprotocol/java-tron, cloned at depth 1). The primary finding is a missing rate-limit (24-hour cooldown) enforcement in the TVM native contract `WithdrawRewardProcessor`, which allows smart contracts to harvest delegation rewards without the cooldown that top-level `WithdrawBalanceContract` transactions enforce. A secondary finding is an integer overflow risk in `VoteRewardUtil.adjustAllowance` and `MortgageService.adjustAllowance`.
**VULN-001 (Medium)**: `WithdrawRewardProcessor.validate()` at `actuator/.../nativecontract/WithdrawRewardProcessor.java:21-36` enforces only GP exclusion but misses the 24-hour `latestWithdrawTime` cooldown check that `WithdrawBalanceActuator.validate()` enforces at `actuator/.../WithdrawBalanceActuator.java:121-128`. Exploitable via TVM `withdrawReward` opcode (Program.java:2329) and `selfdestruct` (Program.java:697), both of which use the same `VoteRewardUtil.withdrawReward()` path without cooldown enforcement.
**VULN-002 (Low)**: `VoteRewardUtil.adjustAllowance()` at `actuator/.../vm/utils/VoteRewardUtil.java:118` and `MortgageService.adjustAllowance()` at `chainbase/.../MortgageService.java:256` use raw `allowance + amount` without overflow check. Mitigated by the `allowance <= 0` guard in `WithdrawRewardProcessor.execute()`, but allows allowance state corruption on overflow
